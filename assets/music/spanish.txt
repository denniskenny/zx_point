================================================================================
 Spanish Ladies (Farewell To Spain) — Tritone arrangement (Game Over screen)
================================================================================
Waltz in E minor, played slow (~60 BPM) as a funeral dirge.  Source ABC:
design.md "Game Over: Spanish Ladies (Farewell To Spain) — Slowed".
Chords: Em|Em|C|D|Em|Am|D|D|G|Am|Em|Em|C|D|Bm|Em.

KEY / ARRANGEMENT
  * Key: E minor (Emin) => F is F#; all other notes natural.
  * Meter: 3/4 waltz, L=1/8, so each bar = 6 eighth-notes -> 6 rows.
  * Octaves (ABC -> pitch):  X, = octave 3,  X = octave 4.  Lead sits A3-B4,
    Harmony a third below, Bass root-5th-5th in octave 3.
  * Bass waltz pattern: root (beat 1) then 5th (beats 2,3).
  * The 2-eighth anacrusis (Lead B3 / Harm G3) is folded into BAR 16 rows 4-5
    so the tune starts on the downbeat and the pickup leads back in on loop.
  * Notes ring until the next attack (Tritone sustain) -> ties need no markup.

TEMPO
  Q:1/4 = 60 BPM (dirge).  6 rows/bar (1 row = 1/8 note).  One eighth at
  60 BPM quarter = 0.5 s = 25 frames.
rows  = 6
speed = 25
